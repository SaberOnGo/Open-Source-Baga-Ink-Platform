# Baga Ink Commercial Licensing

Baga Ink is developed in public for users, individual developers, researchers, educators, community contributors, device porters, and commercial device makers.

The default community license for Baga-authored Platform/OEM-side software is the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** unless a file or directory explicitly says otherwise.

Commercial OEM/device/platform use is governed by a separate written commercial agreement. A commercial agreement does **not** necessarily imply a platform royalty: qualified device makers that materially expand the Baga Ink device ecosystem may be offered **no-fee or reduced-fee Platform commercial licensing** for a defined scope.

Typical commercial-license cases include:

- OEM preinstallation of Baga Ink Platform;
- shipment or sale of commercial devices containing Baga-authored Platform or Device Adapter code;
- commercial redistribution of Baga Platform binaries or source;
- proprietary OEM integration based on Baga-authored Platform/Adapter code;
- paid managed device/platform deployments using Baga-authored Platform code;
- commercial compatibility/certification arrangements or licensed use of controlled Baga marks where applicable.

## OEM Enablement Program

Baga Ink wants device vendors to have a strong reason to port their hardware into the ecosystem rather than paying a fee merely for helping the ecosystem grow.

A device maker that independently funds, implements, tests, and maintains a conforming Baga Platform Port / Device Adapter may be eligible for a **$0 or reduced-fee Platform commercial license** for specifically agreed device models, firmware ranges, Platform versions, territories, or distribution scope.

Eligibility may consider whether the OEM:

- implements and maintains the required Device Adapter / Platform Port;
- provides reproducible compatibility evidence for the agreed device combinations;
- passes the required Adapter Contract Tests and applicable BICTS profiles;
- fixes regressions or keeps compatibility records current when firmware changes;
- complies with all applicable third-party licenses;
- does not claim official `Baga Ink Compatible` status or use controlled Baga marks without the applicable certification/brand authorization.

A no-fee or reduced-fee Platform license is **not automatic** and does not create a perpetual free license for every future device, firmware, product line, service, or Baga release. The applicable scope is defined in the written commercial agreement.

## What may still be paid

Even where the Platform commercial license is no-fee, separate commercial services may remain paid, including:

- Baga-provided Device Adapter / Platform Port engineering;
- OEM customization and integration work;
- compatibility investigation and performance tuning;
- official certification and use of controlled compatibility marks;
- official Baga Ink Client integration or enterprise device-management work;
- official Market / Repository / hosted-service integration;
- support, maintenance, SLA, white-label, or managed-service arrangements.

The intent is to avoid charging an OEM simply for doing useful ecosystem porting work, while allowing Baga Ink to charge for additional value and official commercial services.

## App developers

Building or selling an IKP application that merely targets the published Baga app APIs does **not by itself** require a Baga OEM/platform commercial license.

App-facing sample code and SDK components may be released under separate permissive licenses; always follow the license notice attached to the specific file/directory.

## Evaluation

Commercial organizations interested in evaluation, prototyping, OEM integration, or device bring-up should contact the project before relying on the noncommercial community license. Evaluation licensing may be offered separately and may be free of charge.

## Official Client / Market boundary

The official **Baga Ink Client**, official Market/Repository services, signing/trust infrastructure, certification services, compatibility branding, hosted services, and related commercial products are separate from the public Platform protocol surface and may remain proprietary or commercially controlled.

Public distribution protocols may allow compatible third-party tooling to exist, but implementing a public protocol does not automatically grant access to official Baga services, trust roots, commercial accounts, LifeBook distribution rights, certification, or trademarks.

## Commercial terms

Commercial pricing is intentionally not fixed in this repository. Terms may depend on device-port contribution, shipment volume, support, customization, certification, territory, hosted services, or other commercial requirements.

For commercial licensing discussions, contact the repository owner / Baga Ink project operator through the contact channel published by the project or the GitHub account `SaberOnGo`.

## Important boundaries

A Baga commercial license applies only to rights the Baga licensor can grant. It does **not** relicense third-party software such as KOReader, koreader-base, FBInk, KPM, KindleTool, or other upstream components. Concrete distributions must independently comply with all applicable upstream licenses.

LifeBook production application code is proprietary and is not included in the public Baga Ink Platform source distribution unless a particular file explicitly says otherwise.
