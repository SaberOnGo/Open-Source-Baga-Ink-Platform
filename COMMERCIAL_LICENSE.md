# Baga Ink Commercial Licensing

Baga Ink is developed in public for users, individual developers, researchers, educators, community contributors, device porters, and commercial device makers.

The default community license for Baga-authored Platform/OEM-side software is the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** unless a file or directory explicitly states otherwise.

Commercial OEM/device/platform use is governed by a separate written commercial agreement. Depending on the applicable OEM Enablement terms, the Platform license fee may be zero or reduced for a defined device/product scope.

Typical commercial-license cases include:

- OEM preinstallation of Baga Ink Platform;
- shipment or sale of commercial devices containing Baga-authored Platform or Device Adapter code;
- commercial redistribution of Baga Platform binaries or source;
- proprietary OEM integration based on Baga-authored Platform/Adapter code;
- paid managed device/platform deployments using Baga-authored Platform code;
- commercial compatibility/certification arrangements or licensed use of controlled Baga marks where applicable.

## OEM Enablement Program

A device maker that independently funds, implements, validates, and maintains a conforming Baga Platform Port / Device Adapter may be eligible for **no-fee or reduced-fee Platform commercial licensing** for an agreed scope.

Eligibility may include:

- implementation and maintenance of the required Device Adapter / Platform Port;
- reproducible compatibility evidence for the covered device combinations;
- passing required Adapter Contract Tests and applicable BICTS profiles;
- maintenance of compatibility across the supported firmware range;
- compliance with all applicable third-party licenses;
- compliance with Baga trademark and certification rules.

The written agreement defines the licensed scope, which may include device models, firmware ranges, Platform versions, territories, product families, distribution channels, support terms, and duration.

No-fee or reduced-fee terms apply only to the scope stated in the agreement and do not automatically extend to future devices, firmware revisions, product lines, services, or Baga releases.

## Separately licensed products and services

The following products and services may be governed by separate commercial terms even when the Platform license fee is zero:

- Baga-provided Device Adapter / Platform Port engineering;
- OEM customization and white-label integration;
- compatibility investigation and performance tuning;
- official certification and compatibility-mark authorization;
- official Baga Ink Client integration or enterprise device management;
- official Market / Repository / hosted-service integration;
- support, maintenance, SLA, and managed-service arrangements.

## App developers

Building or selling an IKP application that targets the published Baga App APIs does **not by itself** require a Baga OEM/platform commercial license.

App-facing SDK source, templates, sample code, and example applications may use separate permissive licenses. The license notice attached to the specific file or directory governs those assets.

## Evaluation

Commercial evaluation, prototyping, OEM integration, or device bring-up can be covered by separate evaluation terms. Evaluation terms may be provided without a license fee.

## Official Client / Market boundary

The official **Baga Ink Client**, official Market/Repository services, signing/trust infrastructure, certification services, compatibility branding, hosted services, and related first-party products may remain proprietary or commercially controlled.

Public distribution or interoperability protocols may permit compatible third-party tooling. Implementing such a protocol does not automatically grant access to official Baga services, trust roots, commercial accounts, LifeBook distribution rights, certification, trademarks, or proprietary service implementations.

## Commercial terms

Commercial pricing is not fixed in this repository. Terms may depend on licensed scope, device-port contribution, shipment volume, support, customization, certification, territory, hosted services, or other commercial requirements.

For commercial licensing discussions, contact the repository owner / Baga Ink project operator through the project contact channel or the GitHub account `SaberOnGo`.

## Important boundaries

A Baga commercial license applies only to rights the Baga licensor can grant. It does **not** relicense third-party software such as KOReader, koreader-base, FBInk, KPM, KindleTool, or other upstream components. Concrete distributions must independently comply with all applicable upstream licenses.

LifeBook production application code is proprietary and is not included in the public Baga Ink Platform source distribution unless a particular file explicitly states otherwise.
